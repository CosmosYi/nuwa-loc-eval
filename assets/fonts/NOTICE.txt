NUWA Display (nuwa-display-regular.woff, nuwa-display-bold.woff)

These files are renamed Latin subsets of TeX Gyre Adventor Regular and Bold,
made with fontTools from the copies in TeX Live
(texmf-dist/fonts/opentype/public/tex-gyre/texgyreadventor-*.otf).

TeX Gyre Adventor is (C) 2006-2008 B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki,
GUST e-foundry, and is distributed under the GUST Font License
(https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/licenses).
As the licence asks for modified versions, the subset carries a different
font name.
